Important: This policy explains the intended privacy practices of Ethio Call. The app operator must make sure that the practices described here match the live app, its servers, backups and service providers before publishing this page.

1. Who operates Ethio Call?

Ethio Call is operated by its independent developer. For privacy questions or support, contact:

Email: mirexf2024@gmail.com

2. Information the app may process

To provide account access and communication features, Ethio Call may process the following information when you use the relevant features:

  • Account and verification information: phone number and one-time verification codes sent through SMS or Telegram, together with account identifiers needed to authenticate you.
  • Profile information: display name, profile photo and other details you choose to add.
  • Communications: messages, photos, files and voice messages that you choose to send or receive.
  • Call information: call connection and technical information needed to establish and maintain voice or video calls.
  • Device and technical information: information such as device, app, network and diagnostic details where needed for security, troubleshooting and operation.
  • Optional feature information: information you provide when using rewards, wallet, payment or referral features, if those features are available in your version of the app.

3. Microphone and camera

Ethio Call requests microphone and, for video calls, camera access so that you can communicate. Live voice and video calls are not recorded by Ethio Call. Voice messages are recorded by you when you choose to create and send one. Do not share information in a call or message unless you are comfortable sharing it with the recipient.

4. How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, verify sign-ins, deliver messages and files, connect calls, provide app features, respond to support requests, prevent abuse, and maintain and improve the service.

5. Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the app, such as hosting, database, authentication, SMS or Telegram verification, and network communication providers. They may process information only as needed to provide their services. Ethio Call does not sell personal information. The operator should identify the actual providers used by the live app and ensure appropriate privacy terms are in place.

6. Storage and security

Information is stored on systems used to operate Ethio Call. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect it. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. The operator should document the actual hosting locations, retention periods and backup practices.

7. Account deletion

You can request deletion directly in the app by going to Settings → Delete my account and confirming your choice. When account deletion is completed, the account information, messages, photos and voice messages associated with that account are intended to be deleted from the service. Some limited records may remain temporarily in protected backups or where retention is required for security or legal reasons, and should be deleted according to the service's retention schedule.

If you need help, contact mirexf2024@gmail.com.

8. Children and age

Ethio Call is intended for general use. If you are under the age at which you can legally consent to online services in your country, use the app only with the involvement and permission of a parent or legal guardian. Parents or guardians who have questions about a child's information can contact the support email above.

9. Your choices

You can choose what profile information to add and what messages, files or voice messages to share. You can manage app permissions, including microphone and camera access, through your device settings. You can delete your account using the in-app option described above.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Please review it from time to time.
